# StealthBridge Community Code of Conduct

StealthBridge is a public, privacy-conscious financial engineering project. Respectful, technically rigorous collaboration matters.

## Our commitments

We welcome participation regardless of experience, background or identity. Contributors and maintainers must remain professional across issues, reviews and other project spaces.

### Expected

- Discuss evidence, tests and designs rather than attacking people.
- Give constructive feedback; acknowledge newcomers and credit original authors.
- Protect personal privacy and handle security details responsibly.
- Respect intellectual property and external software licenses.
- Disclose material conflicts of interest in funding or technical decisions.

### Unacceptable

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, publishing private keys or personal financial data, fabricated audit/security claims, intentional abuse of deployed systems or intimidation of reviewers are not welcome.

## Reporting and enforcement

For a conduct concern, reach organization maintainers privately through a verified non-public channel. Use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for vulnerabilities. Maintainers may ask for edits, remove content or limit participation as needed to protect the community.

A dedicated conduct contact and formal appeals process will be published as governance matures.
