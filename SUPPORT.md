# Getting Support

StealthBridge is in research and engineering development, not a functioning remittance or fiat payment service.

For development support, read the [organization profile](profile/README.md) and the relevant repository's README. Search [StealthBridge issues](https://github.com/search?q=org%3Astealthbridge-labs+is%3Aissue&type=issues) before creating a new one. Use the appropriate [frontend](https://github.com/stealthbridge-labs/stealthbridge-frontend/issues), [backend](https://github.com/stealthbridge-labs/stealthbridge-backend/issues), [contracts](https://github.com/stealthbridge-labs/stealthbridge-contracts/issues) or [SDK](https://github.com/stealthbridge-labs/stealthbridge-sdk/issues) tracker.

Include sanitized reproduction steps and environment/toolchain versions. Never publish wallet seed phrases, private notes, transaction secrets, KYC or provider credentials.

We cannot process real transfers, recover funds, arrange fiat payouts, verify identity or promise a commercially available corridor. For vulnerabilities, follow the [private reporting policy](SECURITY.md).
